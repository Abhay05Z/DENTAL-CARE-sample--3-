About Dr. Mitchell

Get to know the dentist behind the smile

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS

Cosmetic & General Dentist

Dr. Sarah Mitchell has been at the forefront of dental innovation for over 15 years. After graduating with honors from Harvard School of Dental Medicine, she completed her residency at Johns Hopkins Hospital, where she specialized in cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

Her passion for creating beautiful, healthy smiles has earned her recognition as one of New York's top dentists. She believes that every patient deserves personalized care in a comfortable, state-of-the-art environment.

When she's not transforming smiles, Dr. Mitchell enjoys volunteering with dental outreach programs, hiking, and spending time with her family.
